Know your options

Health, term & personal accident — explained simply.

Three different covers, three different jobs. Here's what each one actually protects, and the common types you'll be choosing between.

Health Insurance

Covers hospitalisation & treatment costs

Pays for hospital bills, surgeries, and medical treatment so a health emergency doesn't drain your savings. Includes individual, family floater, and group/corporate options.

Common types
  • Individual health plan
  • Family floater (shared cover for the whole family)
  • Group / corporate health plan
  • Senior citizen health plan
  • Critical illness cover
  • Maternity & newborn cover
  • Top-up / super top-up plans
Term Insurance

Replaces your income for your family

A pure life cover that pays a lump sum to your family if you're not around — cover available from ₹50 Lakh up to ₹50 Crore depending on your needs.

Common types
  • Level term plan (fixed cover)
  • Increasing / decreasing cover plan
  • Term plan with return of premium
  • Term plan with riders (critical illness, accidental death)
  • Group term plan (via employer)
Personal Accident Insurance

Covers injury, disability & accidental death

Pays out for accidental death, permanent or partial disability, and related medical costs — a low-cost add-on to your protection plan.

Common types
  • Individual personal accident cover
  • Family personal accident cover
  • Group personal accident (via employer)
  • Disability & income-protection riders
Real-world example — Term Insurance

Suresh, 38, in Vijayawada is the sole earner for his family with an outstanding ₹40 lakh home loan and had no term cover at all. A ₹1 Crore term plan, costing less than ₹15,000 a year, would ensure his family could clear the loan and maintain their lifestyle if anything happened to him — instead of losing the home along with him.
